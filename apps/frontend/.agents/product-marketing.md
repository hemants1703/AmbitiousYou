# Product marketing context — AmbitiousYou

Use this file before writing or revising marketing, SEO, or AI-visibility copy.

## Product

**AmbitiousYou** is a free web app for long-term goal tracking. Each ambition combines tasks, milestones, notes, deadlines, and priorities — with a dashboard that recalculates progress, tracks momentum, and shows honest day-streaks. Private by default, no credit card required.

## Positioning

- Identity: "AmbitiousYou is where ambitious people run their ambitions."
- Tagline: "Where ambitious goals become inevitable outcomes"
- Audience: people who think in ambitions, not errands (students, professionals, founders, personal growth)
- Category: ambition / long-term goal management — not a daily todo inbox or team PM tool

## CTAs (keep consistent)

- Join the ambitious ones
- Claim your first ambition / Claim your ambitions
- Declare your ambition

## Do claim

- Free to use; no credit card; no artificial ambition caps
- Private by default (no social / sharing features)
- Tasks + milestones mixed per ambition; progress recalculates automatically
- Momentum charts (7/14/30 days), year activity calendar, honest day-streaks
- Responsive web app in modern browsers

## Do not claim

- Native iOS or Android apps (responsive web only)
- Team collaboration, sharing, or social features
- Offline mode or third-party integrations (not yet)
- Replacement for team project management (e.g. Asana) or daily chore inboxes
- Fake social proof, invented ratings, or AggregateRating schema without real reviews

## Competitive framing (honest)

- vs Todoist: they win daily tasks; we win multi-month ambitions
- vs Notion: they win flexible workspaces; we win opinionated ambition structure out of the box
- vs Asana: they win team delivery; we win personal long-term goals
